Who draws it

Esad is the architect and owner of the studio, a B.Sc. from ETH Zürich and the hand behind every line ZweiFach delivers.

He carries a project through strategic planning, the design, the building permit and the execution plans. He weighs what the plot allows, what it will cost and what a bank will fund, so the drawing is sound long before anyone breaks ground.
