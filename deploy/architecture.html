The process

Six phases, one continuous hand.

Feasibility, design, permit and execution all come off the same drawing board. The project is never passed to a new team halfway through.

01.
Feasibility

Strategic planning & feasibility

Strategische Planung

What the plot can carry: zoning, permitted use and a first massing, tested against cost before anything is committed.

02.
Study

Preliminary study

Vorstudien

The concept takes volume. Layouts, orientation and a first cost frame keep the numbers honest while the design is still cheap to change.

03.
Design

Design / building project

Bauprojekt

The design is resolved to permit depth: plans, sections and materials worked into one coherent, buildable whole.

04.
Permit

Building permit

Baugesuch

We assemble and submit the dossier, then carry the dialogue with the authority through to a granted permit.

05.
Tender

Tender & procurement

Ausschreibung

Specifications go out to the trades, tenders are compared and awarded, and the design turns into cost certainty.

06.
Execution

Execution & detail plans

Ausführungsplanung

The working drawings: precise, dimensioned plans resolved down to the last junction, handed to the site.
