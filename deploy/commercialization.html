Who sells it

Dionis is the studio's commercialisation and financing lead, the one who takes a finished design to market and turns drawings into signed sales.

He positions the project, sets a price the area will carry, and guides buyers to the financing that fits them, comparing mortgage models and checking what each household can comfortably afford. When enough units pre-sell off-plan, the bank releases credit and the build can begin.

The standard Swiss check: 5% imputed rate on the mortgage, 1% of the price for upkeep, the part above two thirds amortised over 15 years, capped at 33% of gross income. Banks also expect 20% own funds, of which 10% of the price outside the second pillar. Not a credit decision, Dionis checks the real case free of charge.
