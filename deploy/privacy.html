Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 July 2026 · ZweiFach, Aargau, Switzerland · Swiss FADP / EU GDPR

01Who we are

ZweiFach ("we", "us") is an architecture and real-estate commercialisation studio based in the Canton of Aargau, Switzerland. We are the controller responsible for the personal data processed through this website. Questions: hello@zweifach.ch.

02What we collect

  • Contact data, name, email and the message you send via our contact form or by email.
  • Project data, information you share about a plot or development so we can respond.
  • Technical data, anonymised usage and device information strictly needed to operate and secure the site.

03Why we process it

To reply to your enquiry, to prepare and deliver our services, to meet legal and accounting obligations, and to keep the site secure. We rely on your consent, our legitimate interest, or the performance of a contract, as applicable.

04Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (e.g. hosting, email), bound by confidentiality, and where partners such as visualisation studios are strictly necessary to deliver a project.

05Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose collected or as required by Swiss law, then delete or anonymise it.

06Your rights

You may request access, rectification, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to certain processing. Write to hello@zweifach.ch. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

07Cookies

This site uses only the cookies necessary for it to function. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are set without your consent.
