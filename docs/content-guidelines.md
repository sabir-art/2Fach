# Content guidelines

## Writing / copy rules (IMPORTANT — important)
- **Never use dashes as punctuation in any visible copy or content.** No em-dashes (—), no en-dashes (–), and no " - " hyphen-as-connector. These are not used. Use commas, periods, colons, or restructure the sentence. (Hyphens inside real compound words like "permit-ready" or "B.Sc." are fine; ranges should be written as "1 to 6", not "1–6".)
- **Do not put internal explanations or corrections into the site copy.** When something is explained internally (e.g. "he does the plans, not the construction", "drawn not built"), that is context to inform the writing — it is NOT text to display, and NOT something to lecture a first-time visitor about. Present the subject's work naturally and positively (what they DO), never defensively (what they don't do) unless the user explicitly asks for that framing.
